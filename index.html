Find the Bunny 🐰

Find hidden bunnies. Bring illustrations to life. Build your own magical storybook.

Find the Bunny is a relaxing hidden-object puzzle adventure. Search intricate black-and-white drawings, uncover adorable hidden bunnies, and watch each scene bloom with color. Every completed illustration becomes a keepsake page in your Bunny Storybook.
